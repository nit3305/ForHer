# ForHer visual direction

## Structural DNA

**Keepsake box chapter gates.** The five existing stages remain distinct gifts, while every surface feels like it belongs to one handmade memory box.

## Reference pull

- Artifact Uprising: photo-first layouts, warm printed color, and heirloom-scale whitespace.
- MILK Books: presentation-box sequencing and the feeling of revealing a crafted physical object.
- Papier: delightful stationery details and approachable personalization.
- Notebook Therapy: soft collectible palettes, restrained seasonal doodles, and gentle rounded forms.
- Present & Correct: postage, labels, paper ephemera, and graphic restraint.
- LoveBook: direct, specific romantic language, used here without its commercial step-by-step layout.

## System

- Palette: cream `#fff8ef`, ivory `#fffdf8`, baby pink `#f8dfe4`, blush `#efc7cc`, peach `#f5cdbb`, dusty rose `#b85f72`, muted red `#914557`, warm ink `#4b3031`.
- Type: Georgia for romantic display, Segoe UI for readable copy, Segoe Print/Bradley Hand as short annotation accents only.
- Recurring object: one tiny hand-drawn heart, supported by tape, stamps, pressed flowers, and pencil marks.
- Navigation: the existing five-dot journey indicator, reinterpreted as scrapbook page tabs. No conventional site navigation.
- Voice: affectionate, slightly shy, and handmade. It may be cute, but never infantilizing or generic. Unknown relationship details remain explicit placeholders.
- Motion: direct manipulation remains primary. Decorations stay quiet; reduced motion preserves every reveal without ambient movement.
- 3D/Spline: intentionally omitted. Remote 3D conflicts with the lightweight paper-object brief; deterministic CSS/SVG ephemera is the correct fallback.

## Stage rhythm

1. Secret note tucked into a cream card.
2. Photograph puzzle on a warm scrapbook worktable.
3. Six different photo-album spreads.
4. A small box of envelopes, stickers, and handwritten reasons.
5. Mixtape → live time card → photo strip → physically unfolding letter → quiet signature.

## Banned moves

- Predominantly dark presentation, glass-dashboard surfaces, giant corporate headings, uniform card grids, neon, or decorative clutter.
- New stages beyond five, autoplay audio, camera access, external 3D runtimes, or changes to the 5×5 jigsaw mechanics.

## Render checkpoint

Verify Stage 1, each Stage 3 spread, every Stage 4 reveal, the Stage 5 finale flow, and the ending at desktop, 390px, and narrow mobile. Decorations must disappear before content compresses, and horizontal overflow must remain zero.
